Website Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026

Overview

This policy covers jamdailytools.com, the public website of JAM Daily Tools LLC ("we," "us"). This is a static informational website about our company and our apps. It has no user accounts, no sign-up forms, and no shopping cart.

Our individual apps have their own privacy policies. If you use one of our products, that product's policy governs the data it collects — for example, TourneySmith's policy is at tourneysmith.com/privacy.

Information we collect on this website

We do not collect personal information through this website. We do not use cookies, advertising, or analytics/tracking scripts, and we do not build profiles of visitors.

Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) automatically processes standard technical request data — such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested — to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. This is handled under Cloudflare's own terms and is not used by us to identify you.

If you email us

If you contact us at admin@jamdailytools.com or support@jamdailytools.com, we receive your email address and the contents of your message and use them only to respond to you. We do not sell or share this information.

Third parties

This website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Links to external sites (such as app stores or our product sites) are governed by those sites' own privacy policies.

Children's privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email admin@jamdailytools.com.
